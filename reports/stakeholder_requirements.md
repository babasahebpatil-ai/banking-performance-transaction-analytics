# Stakeholder Requirements

## Lending / portfolio stakeholders

Typical stakeholders: lending operations, branch managers, portfolio managers, finance, collections, and BI.

Key questions:

1. How many loan records and unique clients are in the portfolio?
2. How many unique clients are currently active?
3. What are total loan value, funded amount, and average loan size?
4. What is the recorded principal recovery rate?
5. What are the default and delinquency rates?
6. What share of repayments are on time?
7. Which branches carry the most value?
8. Which branches show weaker quality indicators?
9. Which products/purposes drive loan value and interest contribution?
10. How does loan value change over time?
11. How do credit-score and employment segments differ?
12. Which delinquent records should operations review first?

## Transaction operations stakeholders

Typical stakeholders: banking operations, branch managers, finance, BI, and manual review teams.

Key questions:

1. What are total credit, debit, and net flow?
2. How many unique customers and accounts are active in the data?
3. What is the average transaction amount and transactions-per-account rate?
4. Which methods drive count and value?
5. Which banks and branches drive activity?
6. Which branches are growing or declining month-over-month?
7. Are monthly periods complete?
8. Which customers drive the most transaction value?
9. Which transactions exceed the governed high-value review threshold?
10. Where are high-value review records concentrated?
