# Business Problem

## Company-style scenario

BankSphere is presented as a simulated retail-banking analytics environment. Management has two reporting problems:

### 1. Lending performance

The lending team has loan-level data but needs consistent definitions for client counts, funding, recovery, delinquency/default indicators, branch performance, and product/purpose performance.

**Management question:** Which parts of the portfolio deserve deeper operational review, and which KPIs should be monitored consistently?

### 2. Transaction operations

The transaction operations team needs a reliable view of credit/debit flows, bank/branch activity, transaction methods, customer/account activity, monthly trends, and high-value records requiring manual review.

**Management question:** How is transaction activity distributed across customers, accounts, banks, branches, methods, and time, and where should operations drill down?

## Business impact

Inconsistent KPI definitions can lead to incorrect management conclusions even when the underlying dashboard is visually strong. The project therefore emphasizes **metric governance, data quality, SQL analysis, and Power BI decision support** rather than unsupported fraud or profitability claims.
